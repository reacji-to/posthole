Uses each site's own API with your signed-in session, spaced out to stay under its rate limits. X and LinkedIn may limit accounts that read very large volumes.

Every 5 minutes, new saves are committed and pushed to origin, and a workflow is installed that publishes the card site (with a SQLite index and embeddings) to GitHub Pages every 15 minutes. Files over 100 MB stay on this computer.
